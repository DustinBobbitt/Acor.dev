A spatial operations system—not merely a rack map.

Warehouse Floor models the building, departments, storage assets and their operational records. It combines layout stewardship, stock discovery, pallet workflows, inventory counting and management review while preserving a deliberate boundary between published facility design and customer-owned working state.

Current capability overview

The public demo currently shows only the shallowest layer.

The accessible standalone repository dated October 16 already contains a User/Admin web workspace, a separate layout editor, facility publications, customer working copies, shared audit services and a broad set of spatial operations. The owner reports that this checkout is behind current development, so this page presents it as a verified lower-bound snapshot rather than the final current feature list.

Facility and layout model

Departments occupy a building overview and retain their own orientation, walls and floor color. The editor can add, rename, move, resize and rotate racks and floor items; arrange department footprints; place pallets; preserve stable identities through renames; and apply guarded map updates without overwriting customer-owned layout state.

Stock and pallet operations

The web workspace can search exact or partial product codes, total quantity and open the corresponding rack bay, level, floor zone or pallet. It supports product homes, location details, practical pallet changes, Cut-pallet search and filtered placement workflows.

Annual count and management

Annual audits include personnel, teams and area assignments; count and recount requests; management progress coloring; historical selection; review and paperwork reconciliation. Rack walk sheets and entered walk results connect physical observation back to the map, including a true-size redraw workflow for measured departments.

Shared custody

The system can use a LAN service or SQL Server-backed persistence so multiple workstations see shared changes. Local backups and a rebuildable index support recovery. This is a trusted internal workflow, not an internet identity or authorization system.
